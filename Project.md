🎯 Purpose

The main purpose of this project is to transform raw newspaper customer data into meaningful insights that can help understand:

- Total newspaper sales and revenue
- Customer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Monthly sales trends
- City-wise and gender-wise customer distribution
- Payment method preferences
- Newspaper quantity and billing performance
